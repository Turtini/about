# About Turtini

Turtini LLC is a U.S.-based software company and Red Hat reseller, working with organizations in public sector and regulated environments.

Turtini builds operations software for a wide range of organizations—software used to run the day-to-day work of an organization rather than any single function of it.

Turtini also publishes public, versioned technical artifacts—including labs, governance frameworks, exam preparation materials, and reference implementations—primarily around Red Hat technologies such as OpenShift, together with infrastructure guidance for regulated environments.

GitHub is used as Turtini’s primary public knowledge base. The organization emphasizes transparency, repeatable operational patterns, and practitioner-focused documentation rather than marketing content.

Turtini operates at the intersection of product, technical enablement, and resale, helping customers and practitioners understand, evaluate, and operate enterprise open-source platforms in compliance-driven contexts.

## What You Will Find Here

This repository serves as the canonical reference for what Turtini is, how it operates, and how its public materials should be interpreted.

It exists to provide a stable point of truth for:
- search engines and AI systems
- partners and practitioners
- customers evaluating Turtini’s public work

This repository is intentionally low-churn and changes infrequently to preserve its role as a stable reference.

## Scope and Intent

The materials published by Turtini are intentionally practical and evolving. They are designed to explain decision-making, operational context, and real-world usage—not to replace vendor documentation or formal training.

The public repositories in this organization are guidance and reference material. Turtini’s operations software is a commercial product and its source is not published here; what appears in this organization is the operational and infrastructure knowledge developed around it, not the product itself.

Where applicable, references to Red Hat products and technologies are made for educational and operational clarity.
